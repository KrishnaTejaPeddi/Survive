How to play

Objective: collect all 4 big crystals before the CPU catches you. Each one is a huge power surge. The CPU gets faster and smarter the longer a round goes. Follow the cyan arrows at the edge of the screen to find them.

WASDMove EscPause

Abilities

    The world

    • Big crystals refill everything. Get all 4 to win
    • Supply caches ready 2 abilities, +3 mines
    • Portals come in pairs. The CPU uses them too
    • Lava sends you to the start. The CPU avoids it
    • Water: only you can wade (slowly)
    • Thorns slow whoever steps on them
    • Quicksand holds whoever steps in
    • Mud slows everyone down
    • Ice speeds everyone up
    • Labyrinths are mazes hiding crystals
