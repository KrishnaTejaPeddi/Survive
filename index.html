How to play

Objective: collect all 4 big crystals before the CPU catches you. Each one is a huge power surge. The red CPU never stops hunting you, and the longer a round goes, the faster and smarter it gets. Follow the arrows at the edge of the screen to find the crystals.

Controls

  • WASDMove
  • SpaceMine. Freezes the CPU when it steps on it
  • EPulse. Freezes and slows the CPU nearby
  • FDash. Leap 8 squares over walls and lava
  • QDecoy. A fake you that traps the CPU
  • RPhase. Walk through walls for 3 seconds
  • TTime stop. Freeze the CPU for 3 seconds
  • ShiftOverdrive. Move twice as fast for 4 seconds
  • EscPause

The world

  • Big crystals. Refill every ability and freeze the CPU. Get all 4 to win
  • Portals come in glowing pairs. The CPU uses them too
  • Lava sends you back to the start. The CPU avoids it
  • Mud slows everyone down
  • Ice speeds everyone up
