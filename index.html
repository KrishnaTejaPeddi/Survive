How to play

Survive as long as you can. The red CPU never stops hunting you, and it gets faster every second. Use your tools, explore the world, and never stop moving.

Controls

  • WASDMove
  • SpaceMine. Slows the CPU when it steps on it
  • ESlow pulse. Slows the CPU if it's close
  • FDash. Leap 5 squares forward
  • QDecoy. A fake you the CPU chases
  • EscPause

The world

  • Portals come in glowing pairs. The CPU uses them too
  • Crystals refill your tools and slow the CPU
  • Lava sends you back to the start. The CPU avoids it
  • Mud slows everyone down
  • Ice speeds everyone up
  • Crystal Caves hold the most crystals
